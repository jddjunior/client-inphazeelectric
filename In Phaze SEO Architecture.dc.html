02 — MONEY PAGE TEMPLATE

The service page, built once.

Every service page runs this exact skeleton. Nine pages, one build, and each one outranks a competitor's single paragraph.

ORLANDO · PERMITTED & INSPECTED

Electrical panel upgrades in Orlando

100-amp to 200-amp service, typically finished in one day. Free estimate — and if your panel is fine, we'll tell you that instead.

CALL (407) 599-7777 Free estimate ★★★★★ 4.8 · 801 REVIEWS
BLOCK 02 · SIGNS YOU NEED THIS

Scannable symptom list — breakers tripping, no room for a charger, fuse box, aluminum wiring, scorch marks. Captures the "is this me?" search.

BLOCK 03 · WHAT IT COSTS

A real table with three tiers and what changes the number. This block alone will out-convert every competitor page in the market.

BLOCK 04 · HOW THE DAY GOES

Hour-by-hour: power off at 8, meter release, new panel set, inspection, back on by dinner. Removes the fear that stalls the decision.

BLOCK 05 · REAL JOBS

Three before/afters with city, amperage, duration. Photo captions carry the keyword naturally — no stuffing required.

BLOCK 06 · MATCHED REVIEWS

Four reviews tagged panel upgrade, pulled live. Fresh text on the page every month with zero editing effort.

BLOCK 07 · PERMITS & CODE

Orange County / City of Orlando specifics, OUC coordination, inspection timeline. Local authority signals Google actively rewards.

BLOCK 08 · FAQ (SCHEMA'D)

Eight real questions in FAQPage markup. Eligible for rich results and for AI answer citation.

BLOCK 09 · CITY LINKS + CTA

"Panel upgrades in Winter Park, Maitland, Lake Mary…" — internal links that build the matrix, then the call block again.

TARGET TERMS

NON-NEGOTIABLES
  • Phone in the first viewport, always
  • 1,200+ words minimum, written by a human

Calls per page, not sessions. Every page carries its own tracked number so you know which page paid for itself.

03 — CITY PAGE TEMPLATE

A city page has to actually know the city.

The current location pages are the same paragraph with the town name swapped — which is exactly what Google discounts. Each page needs four things only a local operator could write.

SEMINOLE COUNTY · SAME-DAY CALLBACK

Electrician in Winter Park, FL

88 Winter Park homeowners have reviewed us. We're eight minutes from Park Ave and we know what's behind the walls of a 1940s bungalow.

LOCAL PROOF

Reviews filtered to Winter Park, with street-level context. Map pins of recent jobs. This is the block that makes the page unfakeable.

LOCAL HOUSING STOCK

What's actually in these homes: cloth wiring in the historic district, 60-amp fuse boxes, Federal Pacific panels, no grounding. Genuinely useful, genuinely local.

LOCAL PERMITTING

City of Winter Park building department, Duke Energy vs. OUC territory, historic-district review. Nobody else writes this. It's the whole moat.

LOCAL JOBS + CREW

Three photographed jobs in town, and which electrician usually covers the area. "Ask for Mike — he's in Winter Park most Tuesdays."

SERVICE LINKS + CALL

Links to all nine services scoped to the city, then the call block. Builds the matrix from the geo side.

THE RULE

If you could swap the city name and the page would still be true, delete the page. Twelve strong city pages beat forty spun ones — and won't get the whole site penalized.

GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE
  • One post per week, photos from real jobs
  • Every service listed as a product with pricing
  • Q&A seeded with the eight real questions
  • Every review answered, in Tony's voice
  • Geo-tagged photos, 10+ per month
CITATIONS

Three different addresses and two phone numbers are live across Yelp, Manta, HomeAdvisor and BBB right now. Inconsistent NAP is actively suppressing local rank. Clean it first — it's the cheapest win on this list.

04 — CONTENT ENGINE

Four posts a month. Forever. From the truck.

No content calendar brainstorm needed — the crew generates the material every day. Someone just has to write it down. Each format below feeds SEO, social, and GBP from a single job.

"What this cost"

One real job, real invoice range, why. Cost queries have the highest intent in the trade and almost nobody answers them honestly.

"We found this"

The scary photo. Bootlegged grounds, melted lugs, DIY disasters. Warning-label treatment. Shares, saves, and safety authority.

"You don't need this"

The honesty file, written up. A job turned down, money saved. Nothing builds trust faster and no competitor can fake it.

"Florida-specific"

Lightning season, pool bonding, hurricane generator prep, EV rebates. Seasonal, searchable, and defensibly local.
