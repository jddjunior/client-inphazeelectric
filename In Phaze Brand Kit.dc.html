10 — THE PROOF SYSTEM

800 reviews are an asset. Right now they're a screenshot.

Proof has to be plumbed, not pasted. Reviews get pulled live, tagged by service and by city, and rendered wherever a decision is being made — which is how they also start feeding SEO.

01 · CAPTURE

Ask on site, every time

Tech-specific QR card left behind. Review mentions the tech by name — which is exactly what already happens organically, now systematized. Target 35 new reviews/month.

02 · TAG

Sort by service + city

Every review labeled: EV charger / Winter Park / Jason. That tagging is what lets a Lake Mary panel page show Lake Mary panel reviews.

03 · DEPLOY

Proof at every decision

Chip in the header. Matched reviews on every service and city page. Two quotes beside every form. Rating in the email signature and on the estimate PDF.

04 · COMPOUND

Feed the algorithm

Review schema on every page, fresh text monthly, GBP posts weekly. Review velocity and freshness are ranking signals — the proof engine is the SEO engine.

Meet the crew

Real headshots and profiles for Grant, Jason, Mike, Victor, William, Keylin, Josh and Janzie — with each one's review count beside their name. Customers are already asking for them by name; let them.

The honesty file

A running page of jobs turned down and money saved. "We told 41 homeowners their panel was fine this year." Nothing a competitor can copy without actually doing it.

Recovery protocol

Every review under 4★ answered inside 24 hours, by Tony, by name, with a fix. Public repair work is more persuasive than a clean sheet.
