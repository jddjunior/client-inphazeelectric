How much does an electrician cost in Orlando?

Diagnostic service calls start at $129, credited toward the repair. Panel upgrades run $2,400–$4,800, EV chargers $650–$1,900, whole-home surge protection $550–$1,400. Estimates for planned work are free, and we publish the full range on every service page rather than saying "call for pricing."

Do I need a permit to replace an electrical panel in Orange County?

Yes. Panel and service changes require a permit and an inspection in every Central Florida jurisdiction, and the utility has to release and reconnect the meter. We file the permit, meet the inspector, and coordinate with OUC, Duke, FPL, KUA or the City of Winter Park depending on your address.

How fast can an electrician get to my house?

Same-day callback anywhere in our service area, and typically on site within one business day for repair calls. Genuinely unsafe conditions — burning smell, hot outlet, sparking panel — go to the front of the schedule ahead of quoted work.

Do I need a panel upgrade to install an EV charger?

Usually not. A load calculation on your existing panel answers it for certain, and a load-management device can often avoid an upgrade entirely for a few hundred dollars instead of a few thousand. We do the calculation free — and we have talked plenty of people out of upgrades they did not need.

Who do I call for an electrical emergency in Central Florida?

Call (407) 599-7777 — the same number after hours. If you can safely reach the breaker for the affected circuit, shut it off first. Do not submit a form for a burning smell or a sparking panel; a form waits in an inbox and a phone gets answered.

Is In Phaze Electric licensed and insured?

Yes — Florida electrical contractor license ER13014534, with general liability and workers' compensation coverage. Certificates are provided before commercial work starts. All labor carries a one-year warranty and we price match any written quote from another licensed, insured contractor.

What areas does In Phaze Electric serve?

Orange, Seminole and Osceola counties from a shop in Orlando: Orlando, Winter Park, Maitland, Longwood, Lake Mary, Winter Garden, Kissimmee, Oviedo, St. Cloud, Apopka, Sanford and Windermere. Just outside that list? Call and ask — we often go.
